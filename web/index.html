NeuroScreen

A multi-domain, multi-modal computer-aided screening tool for diabetic neuropathy. It fuses standing-balance center-of-pressure analysis with wearable gait, plantar-pressure and heart-rate-variability markers to stage peripheral neuropathy and detect cardiac autonomic involvement.
