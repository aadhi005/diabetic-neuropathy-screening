%================ ABSTRACT ==================

\section{Abstract}

\begin{frame}{Abstract}

\small

Diabetes is one of the most common long-term health conditions worldwide, and diabetic neuropathy is among its most frequent complications. It gradually damages the nerves, affecting a person's sensation, balance and walking. Early detection is important, but it is difficult because symptoms often appear only in the later stages, and conventional diagnostic tests are invasive, expensive and require specialists.

This project proposes a non-invasive, computer-aided screening system that studies a person's standing balance and gait together with physiological signals such as heart-rate variability. Using machine learning, the system aims to identify early signs of diabetic neuropathy and support timely diagnosis, providing a simple and affordable screening aid that can assist clinicians.

\end{frame}

%================ PROBLEM ==================

\section{Problem Statement}

\begin{frame}{Problem Statement}

\begin{itemize}

\item Diabetic neuropathy often remains undiagnosed until symptoms become severe.

\item Standard diagnostic tests (nerve conduction studies, biopsy) are invasive, costly and need specialists.

\item Simple bedside tests detect only advanced neuropathy, not early stages.

\item Balance and gait changes caused by neuropathy are not routinely measured.

\item A low-cost, non-invasive screening method is needed for early detection.

\end{itemize}

\end{frame}

%================ OBJECTIVES ==================

\section{Objectives}

\begin{frame}{Objectives}

\begin{enumerate}

\item Study how diabetic neuropathy affects balance, gait and physiological signals.

\item Use suitable datasets of balance, gait and heart-rate-variability data.

\item Extract meaningful features from these signals.

\item Develop a machine-learning model to distinguish healthy and neuropathic individuals.

\item Build a simple interface to present the screening result.

\item Evaluate the accuracy of the system on the available data.

\end{enumerate}

\end{frame}
